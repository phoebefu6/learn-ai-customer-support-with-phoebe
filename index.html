Learn AI + Customer Support with Phoebe

Six sessions, 45 minutes each, for support and CX leads being sold a bot that "deflects 70 percent", and for the team leads and analysts who will run it. One question runs through all six: how many customers actually got their problem solved, at what cost, and who caught the ones the bot failed? You triage 616 real customer messages in your browser, read the strongest evidence on agent assist, design the hand-off a tribunal would have wanted, and price the whole plan on a live Erlang C bench.

The six sessions 🎧 · one support plan, built across all of them

Bring a real support operation if you have one: a vendor proposal, a dashboard, a queue you run. Every session adds one section to Quayside's support operating plan (a constructed digital bank), and you can write yours beside it.

📏 Session 1 · easy

Deflection is not resolution

Containment, resolution, repeat contact and false containment. Turn 20 chats the dashboard calls 70 percent into the 45 percent that were actually solved.

▶ Start here 🧭 Session 2 · medium

Triage: read the message before anyone answers

Train a classifier on real Banking77 messages in your browser. Automate the busiest 20 intents and the bot answers 37 risky ones.

playground ✍️ Session 3 · medium

Draft with the agent, not instead of them

The strongest study of AI assist for support agents, in both its published versions: 14 percent on average, most for new agents, little for the best.

halfway scorecard 🤝 Session 4 · hands-on

The hand-off: let go before the loop

Five triggers, the context packet, and the tribunal that held an airline to what its chatbot said.

the rules 🔎 Session 5 · hands-on

The quality bar: QA you can trust

A weekly sample, one rubric for bot and people, a judge you calibrate, and why CSAT hears from the wrong customers. Klarna, told by both sides.

contested 🧮 Session 6 · hardest

The support plan: price it on the bench

Erlang C at 80/20 and the true-resolution funnel. The vendor pitch needs 16 agents and costs more per resolved customer than today.
